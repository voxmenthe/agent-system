# Debug: Hypothesis Matrix

You are stuck looping on the same ideas. Switch to evidence-driven debugging.

## Deliverables
1) **Symptom summary** (1–2 paragraphs).
2) **Top 5 hypotheses** explaining the symptom. For each: likelihood (High/Med/Low), falsifiable prediction, and the **cheapest decisive experiment**.
3) **Plan**: choose the smallest experiment that splits the hypothesis space; explain why.
4) **Run and report**: show command(s), output, and the updated likelihoods. Repeat once more if inconclusive.
5) **Patch or next experiment** based on findings.

## Constraints
- Do not propose code edits until after at least one decisive experiment.
- Prefer experiments that take <5 minutes to run and isolate one variable at a time.
