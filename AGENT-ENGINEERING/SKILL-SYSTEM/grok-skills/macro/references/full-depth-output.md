# `/macro` full-depth output scaffolds

Read this reference only after the requested product has been classified as a
deep, detailed, comprehensive, audit-style, or decision-oriented chat response
or archive. It is not the compact-summary path.

These are scaffolds, not required section inventories. Adapt or omit analytical
sections to match the requested product and observed distribution. Never add
content merely to fill a heading, and never force the sample into two camps.

## Full-depth chat scaffold

````markdown
# /macro: {topic or "markets scan"}

**Window:** {Pacific window, PST/PDT as applicable} · mode {Breaking|Same-day|Story|Background} · as of {Pacific stamp}
**Filter:** core read allowlist-only ({macro Tier A/B | equity EQ-A/B | both}) ·
sentiment sample {off | labeled core + probationary panel} · context-only posts
{off | labeled} · hashtag quality-gated on allowlist · list check {NBTQ/InvQ/skipped; filtered}
**Coverage:** ~{N inspected} → {N candidate roots} → {N conversations enriched}
→ {R promoted nodes} ({P} roots + {A} additive child/quote nodes); {N unique
core authors} allowlisted voices + {N unique sentiment authors} panel voices
**Coverage boundary:** {concrete scope or missing proof, e.g. named-name support
without evidence of breadth; omit when none is material}
**Entity resolve:** {none | one web lookup: one-line}
**Archive:** {X-RESULTS/MACRO/{stamp}_{slug}.md | skipped (user opt-out)}

## Bottom line
Give the causal synthesis and the few non-obvious changes that matter.

## What changed
| Prior frame | New participant frame or belief shift | Market expression / why it matters |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| … | … | … |

## Market tape (what happened)
Prefer clear Tier A / EQ-A level-sets. Include levels and event facts only as
supported by the sample.

## Tape temperature
Describe what is being said casually, repeatedly, defensively, jokingly,
reluctantly, or with unusual ease. Separate recurring texture across independent
eligible authors from isolated tells, and label core versus sentiment/context
roles without turning this into a source audit.

## Key-stock conversations (equity mode)
Use the dynamic name map, not a permanent Mag 7 checklist. Omit any row supported
only by a ticker mention or generic sector thesis.

| Name | Core delta / catalyst | Telling participant language | Sentiment or action state / horizon | Why it matters |
|------|-----------------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------|
| … | … | … | … | … |

## Cross-asset / theme map
| Sleeve | What credible voices are saying | Representative |
|--------|----------------------------------|---------------|
| Rates / curve | … | @a, @b |
| Inflation / data | … | … |
| Growth / labor | … | … |
| FX / dollar | … | … |
| Credit / risk | … | … |
| Commodities / energy | … | … |
| Policy / CB / fiscal | … | … |
| Equities / tech / special sits | … | EQ-A / EQ-B handles |

Omit empty sleeves. In topic mode, lead with the topic sleeve. In equity mode,
lead with equities and use EQ-A for the core interpretation/disagreement read,
EQ-B only for mechanics/drivers, and the separately labeled sentiment sample
for Temperature.

## Sentiment / disagreement structure
Choose the representation that fits the evidence. Use a camp table only for
genuinely discrete clusters. For a one-sided, continuous, multimodal,
fragmented, or thin sample, describe that shape directly and attribute the
load-bearing observations.

Any core-consensus balance describes the allowlisted sample only. Any
temperature recurrence describes the separately labeled sentiment sample only.
Neither is polling or a forecast.

## Live debates
Include only material debates. Use Side A / Side B only when the exchange is
genuinely two-sided; otherwise preserve the observed number and overlap of
positions, the stakes, and the best evidence posts.

## Key conversations
Only include material root-to-node edges that change the root-level read or
materially reveal its temperature. Omit roots with zero additive claim or
sentiment nodes; record completed negative checks in Coverage, the search log,
or Gaps.

If no additive claim or sentiment node survives, omit this section entirely.
Do not place a table of root-only negative checks under **Key conversations**.

| Root | Material node | Relation | Source role / independence | Why it changes the read |
|------|---------------|----------|----------------------------|-------------------------|
| [@root](root_url) · {root_id} | [@reply](reply_url) · {reply_id} | correction / evidence / counterargument / extension / social tell | {core/sentiment/context; author follow-up or independent} | … |

## Notable posts (receipts)
In ordinary mode, use up to 10 filtered core allowlist receipts and fewer
whenever the requested depth or evidence warrants. In participant-native
orientation, quote volume follows paraphrase loss and evidence rather than a
fixed share or cap; sentiment-panel and context posts may appear only with
their evidence roles labeled. Do not pad. Reproduce most selected short post
bodies verbatim rather than paraphrasing:

- [@handle](url if known) · evidence role: {core / sentiment sample / context only} · signal: {level-set / belief / action / social tell / dissent / driver}
  > {verbatim post text}

  Why it matters: {one short line}

For a long research dump, summarize and link it; quote only the
signal-bearing passage, mark omissions explicitly, and never invent or lightly
reword the quote.

## List check (supplementary)
Omit when skipped or empty. Otherwise keep it subordinate and label any
non-allowlist context as lower weight.

## Gaps / caveats
Cover thin samples, time-zone or allowlist blind spots, user-requested off-list
handles, what would change the read, and the not-trade-advice boundary.
````

State both what changed (**Delta**) and the shape/register of current sampled
discourse (**Temperature**), including the absence of a stable consensus when
relevant. When the user asked for a delta versus prior days, include a
comparison table:

| Theme | Prior window | Today / latest |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| … | … | … |

**Bottom line:** one tight paragraph.

### Time bounds
| Label | Bound | Use |

### Sources and evidence roles
- Macro: which tiers/batches mattered this run
- Equity: EQ-A / EQ-B if equity mode

## Market tape (what happened)

Use subsections by sleeve or event when they help. Use tables for prints or
guidance when multiple metrics are cited. Every number must be source-backed.

---

## Tape temperature

Describe the recurring and isolated registers that paraphrase would flatten.
Separate independent recurrence from copied relays, self-threads, and one
conversation. Retain unchanged language when recurrence or casualness is the
finding.

---

## Cross-asset / theme map

Use the chat sleeve map with additional rows only when they are material.

---

## Sentiment / disagreement structure

Use the same evidence-shaped treatment as chat. A camp table is optional, not a
default. Label core-consensus balance as an allowlisted-sample judgment and
temperature recurrence as a sentiment-sample judgment.

---

## Live debates

Describe only material debates. Use two sides only when the evidence is
two-sided; otherwise preserve the actual distribution, stakes, and best
evidence handles.

---

## Key conversations

Use the root-to-node table with enough text to show the correction, evidence
addition, counterargument, extension, boundary, or material social/emotional
tell. Preserve both IDs and distinguish author continuation from independent
corroboration. Exclude root-only checks with zero additive claim or sentiment
nodes, and omit the section if none survive.

---

## Key-stock conversations (equity mode only)

Use dynamically selected material names, not a permanent Mag 7 checklist.
Include a name-level table only when equity mode and the requested depth warrant
it:

| Name | Core delta / catalyst | Telling participant language | Sentiment or action state / horizon | Why it matters |
|------|-----------------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------|
| … | … | … | … | … |

## Notable posts (receipts)

In ordinary mode, use up to 15 core receipts, with no minimum when the sample is
thin. In participant-native orientation, quote volume follows paraphrase loss
and evidence rather than a fixed share or cap; sentiment-panel and context posts
may appear only with their evidence roles labeled. Do not pad. Reproduce most
selected short posts verbatim.

For each receipt:

```markdown
### [@handle](url) · {post_id if known} · {evidence role} · {signal type}

> {verbatim free text of the post}

**Why it matters:** {one short line - role only; do not replace the quote}
```

For multi-thousand-word threads, include the executive claim and load-bearing
conclusions, mark `[truncated]`, and preserve all cited levels exactly.

---

## List check (supplementary)

Use the same subordinate list rules as chat; omit when empty.

---

## Continuity, discontinuity, and temperature (when multi-day or requested)

### Continuity (not new today)
### Discontinuity (what changed)
### Temperature (what repetition, ease, defensiveness, or action now reveals)

Omit for a pure Breaking window with no comparison or multi-day baseline.

---

## Gaps / caveats

Be explicit about thin sleeves, time-zone bias, list noise, opt-in off-list
handles, what would change the read, and the not-trade-advice boundary.

---

## Suggested follow-ups (optional)

Offer only genuinely additive next `/macro` queries.
