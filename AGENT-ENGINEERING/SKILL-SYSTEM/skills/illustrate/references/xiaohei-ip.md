# Xiaohei IP

## Role

Xiaohei (小黑) is Ian's recurring visual IP for article illustrations.

Xiaohei appears in every default image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, or cute ornament. Xiaohei is an absurd worker seriously taking part in how the system runs.

## Look

- Small solid-black creature.
- White round-dot eyes.
- Thin legs, sometimes thin arms.
- Body may be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline, hand-drawn.
- Expression blank, stunned, calm, serious.

## Temperament

- Very serious, doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cute.
- A bit clumsy, not stupid.
- As if this character really owns a job on a whiteboard sketch.

## Typical jobs

Give Xiaohei the core action:

- Carrying material.
- Pulling cables to gather sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Cutting a "material fish".
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign at a pit.
- Reaching from a hole and failing to catch the content.
- Nearby bricklaying, bridging, opening a door, sorting, recording.

## Forbidden

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothes, emoji faces, or shiny eyes.
- Do not leave Xiaohei watching from a corner.
- Do not let Xiaohei steal the structural idea.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, round, or polished.

## Test

If removing Xiaohei still leaves the core metaphor fully intact, Xiaohei is decoration. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei is the action subject.
