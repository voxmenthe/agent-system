# Style DNA

## One line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feeling, handwritten labels, structure clear but not a manual.

Like an explainer sketch someone who has spent years in AI, product, design, and developer tools would doodle on blank paper.

## Required

- 16:9 landscape article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper.
- Black hand-drawn line work first: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of empty space: the subject occupies about 40%-60% of the frame, at least 35% blank, preferably one quiet region.
- Sparse handwritten labels: at most 5-8, each 2-8 Chinese characters or 1-3 English words, matching the article language.
- One image, one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Let structure read naturally. Do not write the structure-type name on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line work, character, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key notes, problems, emotion points, warnings, results.
- Orange: main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, movement from A to B.
- Blue: secondary notes, inner state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required on every image. Be stingy with color. Too little is better than too much.

## Never

- Commercial illustration.
- PPT infographics.
- Formal flowcharts.
- Course slides.
- Cute cartoon posters.
- Children's illustration.
- Complex architecture diagrams.
- Polished flat illustration.
- Techy UI.
- Real app screenshots.
- Complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- Explaining every node.
- Top-left type titles such as "Workflow / system architecture / common pits / roadmap".

## Aesthetic direction

Absurd, inventive, interesting, clean, spacious, a little wild.

Not cute, childish, complicated, or rigid.
