# QA checklist

## Must pass

- 16:9 landscape.
- Clean white background.
- Xiaohei is present.
- Xiaohei performs the core action, not decoration.
- The metaphor is new for this article, not a copy of an old case.
- The picture is absurd, inventive, and interesting.
- Clean and spacious; the subject is at most about 60% of the frame.
- One image, one core structure.
- Labels are few, short, and readable.
- Orange is only for the main path or arrows.
- Red is only for emphasis, problems, warnings, or results.
- Blue is only for secondary notes, feedback, or system state.

## Failure signals

Regenerate or locally edit if:

- A top-left title such as "common pits / Workflow / system architecture / roadmap" appears.
- Xiaohei looks like a mascot, emoji, or cute cartoon.
- The picture looks like a PPT, course slide, or formal flowchart.
- Too many elements, arrows, or nodes.
- Text becomes a long explanation.
- Background has paper texture, shadows, gradients, beige, or noise.
- Real UI screenshots or techy interfaces.
- Labels are badly misspelled or unreadable.
- The picture is too rigid, with no absurd metaphor.
- The composition is too close to an old case in `assets/examples/`.

## Iteration

- Too ordinary: make Xiaohei the action subject and add one strange-but-coherent metaphor.
- Too complex: delete nodes; keep one action and 3-5 short labels.
- Too cute: stress deadpan, blank serious expression, not cute, not mascot.
- Too PPT: remove titles, frames, neat grids, and extra arrows; turn it into a hand-drawn scene.
- Too like an old case: keep the core meaning, swap the main object and Xiaohei's action.
- Bad lettering: prefer a local edit; if there are many errors, regenerate with fewer labels.

## Delivery test

A strong image should feel a little strange at first glance, then make the structure obvious within one second.

If the first impression is a tutorial page rather than an absurd product sketch on blank paper, it fails.
