The method is Felix Rieseberg's, from "How to be more ambitious: a tutorial
for humans with models" (13 Oct 2026). The four-question Depth prompt is
his, by way of DannyMac180's prompt-only version; the line about leaving
the user's choices open comes from pstack's `sharpen` (MIT, Lauren Tan).

The local adaptation keeps the request at the centre. The agent infers the
intent behind a request and delivers the request itself above and beyond,
with an outside judge. Anything further comes alongside it: a short,
verified side project, and a bigger version planned as a whole (a spec,
missing inputs to build or reconstruct, what must be learned, and the
working system). The bigger version is suggested, or built when a
multi-session skill is also named. Altitude, Depth and Range become the
tools for that work.

The adaptation also adds a two-way aim check, limits on authority, a
closing report that opens with what was asked and delivered and checks
against gaming the judges, and hand-offs to this skill system's other
skills. It moves the worked examples to a reference. Much of the method's
wording follows the source.
